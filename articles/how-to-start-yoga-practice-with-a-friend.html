How to Start a Yoga Practice With a Friend

Desert Roses Yoga Dance · Published August 20, 2026

Practicing yoga with a friend increases the odds you'll keep showing up, because the commitment is now social, not just personal. Desert Roses Yoga Dance's referral program turns this into a concrete perk: refer 1 friend and unlock a bonus class for both of you, refer 5 for a free month add-on, and refer 10 for a complimentary private session.

Most people who quit a new yoga habit don't quit because the class was bad — they quit because there was no one checking whether they'd show up. A solo commitment is easy to skip on a tired Tuesday. A shared one is harder to break, because skipping it means letting someone else down too.

Why a practice partner changes the odds

This isn't just a nice idea — it shows up consistently in how Desert Roses' own community behaves. Posts and photos of students practicing in pairs, whether a partner backbend on a pier or side-by-side handstands in the studio, come from real students who started together and kept going together. The pattern holds across bellydance, ballet and yoga alike: shared attendance outlasts solo attendance.

The Goddess Referral Challenge

Desert Roses runs a referral program built around exactly this insight — the "Goddess Referral Challenge." It rewards students for bringing people into the studio rather than treating referrals as an afterthought:

  • Refer 1 friend — both of you unlock a bonus class.
  • Refer 5 friends — unlock a free month add-on to your package.
  • Refer 10 friends — unlock a complimentary private session for your group.

How to actually start together

The easiest way to begin a shared practice is to book a free trial class together rather than separately — Desert Roses offers one free first class per new student, so two friends can try the same session on the same day. From there, a Yoga & Dance Combo package (98 SGD for 8 classes over 2 months) or a Yoga package (99 SGD for 10 classes over 3 months) gives enough runway to build a real weekly habit before deciding whether to commit longer-term.

It works for more than yoga

The same logic applies to Bellydance Fitness, Adult Ballet, Chinese Dance and Jazz Dance classes — any class type benefits from a familiar face in the room, and the referral rewards apply across all class types, not just yoga.
