Best Yoga Studio Near Paya Lebar MRT, Singapore

Desert Roses Yoga Dance · Published August 20, 2026

Desert Roses Yoga Dance's Paya Lebar studio is located at 706A Geylang Road, Singapore 389621, convenient to Paya Lebar MRT, and offers the same full class roster as the Chinatown studio: Mandarin Yoga, Bellydance, Adult Ballet, Chinese Dance, Jazz Dance and Yogalates.

Students living or working in the east of Singapore — around Paya Lebar, Geylang, Eunos or Aljunied — often end up choosing a studio based on which one doesn't require crossing half the island after work. The Desert Roses studio at 706A Geylang Road solves that for the east side of Singapore the way the Chinatown studio does for the central area.

One membership, two studios

A real practical advantage of Desert Roses having both a Chinatown and a Paya Lebar location is that a single package works at either studio. That means a student who lives in the east but occasionally works near Chinatown — or vice versa — doesn't need to choose one location and stick with it; classes can be attended at whichever studio fits that week's schedule.

What's taught at Paya Lebar

  • Mandarin Yoga
  • Bellydance Fitness & Choreography
  • Adult Ballet
  • Chinese Dance
  • Jazz Dance
  • Yogalates

Getting started

New students at the Paya Lebar studio get the same free first class as at Chinatown, with no obligation afterward. A Yoga package (99 SGD / 10 classes / 3 months) or Yoga & Dance Combo package (98 SGD / 8 classes / 2 months) both apply across both locations.

A neighbourhood studio, not just a location

Because the Paya Lebar studio has run since the brand's early years, many of its students have practiced together for years — which matters if you're looking for a studio that feels like a community rather than a rotating class schedule.
