Location

The Paya Lebar studio is at 706A Geylang Road, Singapore 389621 — practical for students living or working in the east of Singapore who'd otherwise commute across the island for a class.

Adult Ballet lives here

Adult Ballet — beginner-friendly ballet fundamentals built for adult students, not children's technique scaled down — is currently taught only at this location. If ballet is specifically what brought you here, Paya Lebar is the studio to book.

Why start ballet as an adult

Adult ballet classes are structured around adult joints, adult schedules and adult goals — posture, control and grace — rather than competitive technique. Nobody in the room is being trained for a stage career; most students are there for the same reasons they'd take any other movement class.

Getting there

As with the Chinatown studio, a first class at Paya Lebar is free, so there's no need to commit to a package before deciding whether ballet — or any other class offered here — is the right fit.
