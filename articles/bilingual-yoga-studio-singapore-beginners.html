Bilingual Yoga Studio in Singapore: A Beginner's Guide

Published 2026-02-03 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

A bilingual yoga class simply runs instruction in both English and Mandarin side by side, so you're never left guessing a pose name or cue — it doesn't require you to speak Mandarin, and beginners are welcome at any level of either language.

Why bilingual instruction helps, not hinders, beginners

Most first-time students worry a bilingual class will be confusing — two languages, twice the information. In practice it's the opposite. At Desert Roses Yoga Dance, instructors give each cue in English and Mandarin (English 与 Mandarin 双语教学), so if a pose name or breathing instruction doesn't land the first time in one language, it lands in the second. For Singapore's genuinely mixed-language households, that redundancy is a relief rather than a distraction.

What your first class actually looks like

Classes open with a few minutes of seated breathing, move through a guided warm-up, build through standing and floor postures at a pace instructors adjust to whoever is in the room that day, and close with a short seated or lying wind-down. Nobody is expected to keep up with an advanced student next to them — self-paced practice is the studio's whole philosophy, not a beginner's consolation prize.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothing you can move in — no special yoga wardrobe required.
  • A water bottle. Mats are provided at the studio.
  • Nothing else — arrive 10 minutes early for your first class so an instructor can walk you through anything unfamiliar.

Trying it risk-free

Your first class at either studio location is free, which is deliberately how Desert Roses wants beginners to decide whether the format suits them — by attending, not by reading a brochure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak Mandarin to join a bilingual yoga class?

No. Every cue is given in both English and Mandarin, so English-only speakers follow the class just as easily as Mandarin speakers do.

Is the first class really free?

Yes — new students get one free trial class at either the Chinatown or Paya Lebar studio before deciding on a package.
