Corporate Wellness Workshops in Singapore

Desert Roses Yoga Dance · Published August 20, 2026

Desert Roses Yoga Dance runs corporate wellness workshops and team-building sessions for Singapore companies, combining yoga, bellydance and other class formats into a single group session designed to be genuinely enjoyable, not just a box-ticking HR exercise.

Most corporate wellness sessions fail for a simple reason: they're built to satisfy a wellness budget line, not to actually be a good hour for the team attending. Desert Roses approaches corporate sessions the same way it approaches its regular studio classes — bilingual, beginner-friendly, and paced for the room rather than a fitness ideal.

What a corporate session looks like

Sessions are typically 45–60 minutes and can be built around yoga fundamentals, a lighter bellydance-fitness format for a higher-energy team event, or a mixed format combining both. Because instructors are used to teaching complete beginners at the studio, a corporate group with zero yoga or dance experience is treated exactly the same as a first-time individual student — no one is expected to already know what they're doing.

Why teams respond well to this format

  • Shared, not individual — the whole team experiences the same session together, which builds the same social-commitment effect that keeps regular studio students coming back.
  • Bilingual instruction — useful for Singapore offices with mixed English/Mandarin-speaking staff.
  • No prior fitness level assumed — sessions are genuinely accessible to a team with a wide range of fitness backgrounds.

Where sessions can be held

Workshops can run at either Desert Roses studio — 22B Upper Cross Street in Chinatown or 706A Geylang Road in Paya Lebar — or can be arranged on-site at an office, depending on group size and space.

Booking a corporate workshop

Corporate pricing is quoted directly based on group size, session length and location, rather than a fixed public rate. Message Desert Roses on WhatsApp (+65 9665 2368) or Facebook (desertrosesbellydance) with your team size and preferred dates to get a quote.

A lower-risk way to introduce wellness at work

Because Desert Roses already offers a free first class to individuals, teams unsure about committing to a full corporate program can also send a few employees to a regular free trial class first, to get a feel for the studio's teaching style before booking a group session.

Frequently asked questions

Can corporate wellness sessions be held at our office instead of the studio?

Yes, on-site sessions can be arranged depending on group size and available space — message the studio to discuss your office setup.

Is prior yoga or dance experience needed for a corporate session?

No, sessions are designed to be accessible to teams with any range of fitness backgrounds, the same as a beginner studio class.
