Corporate Wellness Workshops in Singapore

Published 2026-03-31 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

Desert Roses Yoga Dance runs corporate wellness and team-building workshops for companies, quoted per group rather than sold as a fixed package — sessions typically draw on the same bilingual yoga and dance formats taught at the studio, adapted for a one-off group setting.

Why companies book a wellness workshop instead of a generic team event

Team-building events succeed or fail on whether people actually participate rather than stand at the edge of the room. A movement-based session — yoga, or a lighter bellydance/dance format — gives everyone a shared, low-stakes activity that doesn't require prior fitness or dance experience, which lowers the bar for the reluctant half of any team that dreads 'team-building.'

How sessions are structured

Workshops are adapted from the studio's existing bilingual class formats — most commonly a gentle yoga session or an energizing bellydance-fitness format — scaled to a one-off group rather than an ongoing class, and can run on-site at a company's own office or at the studio.

Pricing

Because group size, format, location and session length all vary by company, corporate workshops are quoted directly rather than priced as a fixed package — the fastest way to get a number is a WhatsApp message describing your team size and what you're looking for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fixed price for corporate workshops?

No — corporate wellness workshops are quoted per group based on size, format and location.

Can a workshop run at our own office?

Yes — workshops can be held on-site at a company's office or at the Desert Roses studio, depending on what's requested in the quote.
