Adult Ballet for Beginners in Singapore

Published 2026-03-17 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

Adult beginner ballet classes teach the same foundational technique as children's ballet — posture, barre work, basic positions — but paced and framed for adult bodies and adult goals rather than performance training.

You're not starting 'too late'

The most common hesitation adults have about ballet is timing — the sense that ballet is something you start as a child or not at all. A beginner adult class assumes zero prior training. Everyone in the room started as an adult beginner too.

What a class actually covers

  • Posture and alignment fundamentals — the foundation everything else in ballet builds on.
  • Barre work at a beginner pace, focused on control rather than flexibility extremes.
  • Basic positions and simple center-floor sequences.
  • Breath and body awareness carried over from the studio's broader movement philosophy.

Where to find it

Adult Ballet is currently taught at Desert Roses' Paya Lebar studio, 706A Geylang Road, Singapore 389621. It isn't offered at the Chinatown location, so confirm the studio address when booking.

What to wear for your first class

Comfortable, close-fitting clothing you can move in is enough for a first class — there's no requirement to arrive in ballet-specific attire before you've decided whether the class is for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any dance background to join adult ballet?

No — the class is built for complete beginners, with no prior dance training assumed.
