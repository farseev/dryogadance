Yoga Pricing Guide: Singapore 2026

Published 2026-03-24 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

At Desert Roses Yoga Dance, a 10-class Yoga Package costs 99 SGD (valid 3 months) and an 8-class Yoga & Dance Combo costs 98 SGD (valid 2 months); every new student's first class is free regardless of which package they later choose.

Why validity periods matter more than the headline price

A cheaper package with a short validity window can end up costing more per class if you can't attend often enough to use it — and a slightly pricier package with a longer window can be the better value for a busier schedule. Compare cost per class against your realistic attendance, not just the sticker price.

What's included before you buy anything

Every new student gets one completely free trial class, at either studio location, before committing to a package. That's the intended way to decide which format and pace suits you — not a pricing page.

Corporate workshops are quoted separately

Corporate wellness and team-building workshops are priced per group and quoted directly rather than sold as a fixed package — reach out via WhatsApp for a quote specific to your team's size and format.
