Yoga Pricing Guide: Singapore 2026

Desert Roses Yoga Dance · Published August 20, 2026

At Desert Roses Yoga Dance in Singapore, the first class is free. After that, a Yoga package costs 99 SGD for 10 classes valid 3 months, a Yoga & Dance Combo package costs 98 SGD for 8 classes valid 2 months, and larger Regular Packages range from 498 SGD (30 classes / 6 months) to 999 SGD (78 classes / 12 months, yoga-only).

Yoga pricing across Singapore varies widely depending on studio size, location and whether classes are drop-in or package-based. Here's a direct, current breakdown of what it costs at Desert Roses Yoga Dance, a bilingual studio with locations in Chinatown and Paya Lebar.

How to think about cost-per-class

Working out cost-per-class is the most useful way to compare packages honestly. The entry Yoga package (99 SGD / 10 classes) works out to about 9.90 SGD per class over 3 months. The largest yoga-only package (999 SGD / 78 classes over 12 months) works out to about 12.80 SGD per class, but buys a full year of validity — useful for students who don't want to worry about an expiry date creeping up.

What's included in every package

Every package includes access to studio classes at both the Chinatown and Paya Lebar locations, plus eligibility for the referral program — bring a friend and unlock a bonus class, bring five and unlock a free month add-on, bring ten and unlock a complimentary private session.

No hidden costs

Mats and props are provided at both studios, so there's no separate equipment cost to budget for. Corporate wellness workshops are priced separately and quoted directly based on group size — message the studio for a corporate quote.

Getting started

Start with the free first class before committing to any package. Message Desert Roses on WhatsApp (+65 9665 2368) to book.

Frequently asked questions

What's the cheapest way to try Desert Roses classes?

The first class is completely free for new students, with no obligation to buy a package afterward.

Which package has the lowest cost per class?

The largest yoga-only package (999 SGD / 78 classes / 12 months) has the lowest average cost per class, at roughly 12.80 SGD, though it requires the largest upfront commitment.

Do packages work at both studio locations?

Yes, every package is valid at both the Chinatown and Paya Lebar studios.
