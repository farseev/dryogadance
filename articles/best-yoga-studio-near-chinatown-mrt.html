Best Yoga Studio Near Chinatown MRT, Singapore

Desert Roses Yoga Dance · Published August 20, 2026

Desert Roses Yoga Dance's Chinatown studio is located at 22B Upper Cross Street, Singapore 058334, a short walk from Chinatown MRT station, and offers bilingual (English/Mandarin) Yoga, Bellydance, Adult Ballet, Chinese Dance, Jazz Dance and Yogalates classes.

Finding a yoga studio in central Singapore that's actually easy to reach after work is harder than it should be — most listings look convenient until you check the walk time from the nearest MRT exit. The Desert Roses studio at 22B Upper Cross Street sits within easy walking distance of Chinatown MRT, making it a realistic after-work or weekend option for anyone working or living around the Central Business District, Tanjong Pagar, or the Chinatown / Outram area.

What sets this location apart

Beyond proximity, the Chinatown studio is genuinely bilingual — classes are taught in both English and Mandarin, which is unusual for the area and particularly useful for students who prefer instruction in Mandarin, or who want to bring a parent or grandparent along.

Corporate wellness workshops and team-building sessions can also be arranged at this location for nearby offices.

Trying it out

New students get one free first class at the Chinatown studio, with no obligation to purchase a package afterward. If you continue, a Yoga package is 99 SGD for 10 classes over 3 months, or a Yoga & Dance Combo package is 98 SGD for 8 classes over 2 months.

Bringing a colleague or friend

Because the studio sits so close to the CBD, it's a popular after-work option for colleagues who want to unwind together — and Desert Roses' referral program rewards exactly that: bring one friend and you both unlock a bonus class.
