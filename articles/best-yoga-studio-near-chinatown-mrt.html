Best Yoga Studio Near Chinatown MRT

Published 2026-02-24 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

Desert Roses Yoga Dance's Chinatown studio is at 22B Upper Cross Street, Singapore 058334 — a short walk from Chinatown MRT — and offers Mandarin Yoga, Bellydance Fitness & Choreography, Chinese Dance and Jazz Dance, all taught bilingually.

Location

The studio sits at 22B Upper Cross Street, Singapore 058334, within easy walking distance of Chinatown MRT — convenient for a class before or after work without a detour across town.

What's taught at this location

  • Mandarin Yoga — bilingual instruction, all levels.
  • Bellydance Fitness & Choreography — cardio-driven, beginner-friendly.
  • Chinese Dance — classical and contemporary forms.
  • Jazz Dance — upbeat technique and routines.

Adult Ballet runs at the Paya Lebar location rather than Chinatown — see the linked article below if ballet is what you're after.

What to look for in any studio near an MRT stop

Proximity is only useful if the class itself fits your level. Ask whether a studio runs mixed-level classes (Desert Roses does, at a self-paced rather than leaderboard pace) and whether instruction is offered in the language you're most comfortable in — bilingual English/Mandarin cuts that concern out entirely at this location.
