Yoga vs. Bellydance: Which Should You Try First?

Published 2026-02-17 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

Yoga is the gentler, slower-paced starting point focused on breath, flexibility and calm; Bellydance Fitness & Choreography is higher-energy and cardio-driven, built around rhythm and performance movement — neither requires prior experience, so the real deciding factor is which pace you're drawn to today.

Pace and intensity

Mandarin Yoga runs at a controlled, breath-led pace — holds, transitions, and stillness. Bellydance Fitness & Choreography moves continuously to music, with a genuine cardio component layered under the choreography. If you want to end a class calm, start with yoga. If you want to end a class energized and a little out of breath, start with bellydance.

What each class actually builds

Yoga builds flexibility, breath control and body awareness over weeks of repeated postures. Bellydance builds core strength, coordination and rhythm, with the choreography itself doubling as a fun, low-barrier introduction to dance for people who've never studied it formally.

Neither requires experience

Both classes are taught for real beginners, not dancers or yogis in training. The bilingual instruction (English 与 Mandarin) applies to both formats equally, so language isn't a factor in which one to pick.

Can you do both?

Many students do — the Yoga & Dance Combo package (98 SGD for 8 classes, valid 2 months) exists specifically for students who want to mix formats rather than commit to just one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is bellydance fitness appropriate for beginners with no dance background?

Yes — Bellydance Fitness & Choreography at Desert Roses is taught for first-timers, with no prior dance experience assumed.

Can I try both yoga and bellydance before choosing?

Yes — your first class at either format is free, and the Yoga & Dance Combo package lets you continue mixing both.
