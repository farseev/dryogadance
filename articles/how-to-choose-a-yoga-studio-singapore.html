How to Choose a Yoga Studio in Singapore

Published 2026-04-07 · Desert Roses Yoga Dance

Before committing to a yoga studio, confirm the language of instruction, whether classes are self-paced or performance-paced, real proximity to your commute, exact package pricing and validity, and whether a free trial class is offered — the last one matters most, since it lets you test the other four before paying anything.

Ten questions worth asking any studio

  1. What language is the class actually taught in — and is that a real fit for you, not just a checkbox?
  2. Is the class self-paced, or does it assume you're already at a certain level?
  3. How far is the studio from where you actually spend your time, not just on a map?
  4. What's the exact package price, class count and validity window — not just a per-class 'from' price?
  5. Is there a free trial class before you're asked to commit?
  6. What happens if you miss a class — does it expire, or can it roll over?
  7. Are instructors consistent week to week, or does the teacher rotate unpredictably?
  8. Is the studio clean, well-ventilated and reasonably quiet outside of class hours?
  9. Does the studio offer more than one format, in case your first choice isn't the right fit?
  10. Can you reach a real person quickly (WhatsApp, phone) if you have a scheduling question?

Why the free trial question matters most

A studio confident in its own teaching will let you test everything else on this list — language, pace, distance, instructor style — before asking for money. Desert Roses' free first-class policy at both the Chinatown and Paya Lebar studios exists for exactly this reason.
