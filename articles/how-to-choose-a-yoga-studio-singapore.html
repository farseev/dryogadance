How to Choose a Yoga Studio in Singapore: 10 Questions to Ask

Desert Roses Yoga Dance · Published August 20, 2026

Before committing to a yoga studio in Singapore, ask about language of instruction, free trial availability, package validity periods, whether packages work across multiple locations, class variety, and whether the studio has a referral or bring-a-friend program — all of which affect whether the studio will actually fit your life, not just your budget.

Choosing a yoga studio is less about finding the "best" one in the abstract and more about finding the one that fits how you'll actually use it. Here are ten concrete questions worth asking before you commit.

1. Is instruction available in your preferred language?

If English isn't your first language, or you want to bring a Mandarin-speaking family member, ask whether classes are taught bilingually. Desert Roses has taught in both English and Mandarin since 2009.

2. Is there a free trial class?

A studio confident in its teaching should let you try before you commit financially. Desert Roses offers one free first class to every new student.

3. How long is a package valid for?

Validity periods matter as much as price. A 99 SGD / 10-class Yoga package valid for 3 months is very different from the same price valid for only 4 weeks — check both numbers, not just the price.

4. Does the package work at more than one location?

If you split time between different parts of the city, a package that only works at one studio location limits you. Desert Roses packages work at both its Chinatown and Paya Lebar studios.

5. What class variety is actually offered?

A studio offering only one class format limits how your practice can grow. Desert Roses offers Mandarin Yoga, Bellydance, Adult Ballet, Chinese Dance, Jazz Dance and Yogalates under one membership.

6. Is there a referral or bring-a-friend incentive?

Practicing with a friend measurably improves consistency — a studio that rewards this (like Desert Roses' referral program) is signalling it understands how habits actually form.

7. Are classes competitive or self-paced?

Ask whether modifications are normalized or whether the class expects everyone to move in lockstep — this matters enormously for beginners.

8. How close is it to an MRT station?

A studio that's inconvenient to reach quietly becomes a studio you stop going to. Check the actual walk time from the nearest MRT, not just the neighbourhood name.

9. Does the studio run corporate or group sessions?

If you might want to bring your workplace along later, check whether the studio already runs corporate wellness workshops.

10. How long has the studio been operating?

Longevity is a reasonable proxy for a stable teaching community — Desert Roses has operated in Singapore since 2009.

Frequently asked questions

What's the single most important question to ask a new studio?

Whether there's a free trial class — it lets you answer most of the other questions firsthand before spending anything.

Does package validity matter more than price?

Often yes — a cheaper package with a short validity window can cost more per usable class than a slightly pricier one with a longer window.
